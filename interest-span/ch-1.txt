Imagine meeting someone who seems genuinely interested in you.

They message you first. They ask about your day. They remember little details about your life. Sometimes, they're the first person you speak to in the morning and the last person you talk to at night.

You begin to get comfortable.

You start expecting their messages. You look forward to seeing their name appear on your screen.

And then something changes.

Their replies become shorter. Conversations feel forced. The enthusiasm disappears.

Eventually, you're left wondering what happened.

Did you say something wrong? Did they meet someone else? Were they pretending the entire time?

What's particularly strange is that sometimes, nothing obvious happens before the change.

One person goes from making you feel wanted to making you feel invisible.

And the more you try to understand why, the more confusing it becomes.

But psychology offers some interesting explanations for why this happens.

And some of them have very little to do with you.
