If you've ever experienced this, there's something worth remembering.

Someone losing interest in you doesn't automatically mean you became less interesting, less attractive or less deserving of affection.

Their behaviour might reflect their circumstances, their communication habits, their emotional readiness or simply a change in what they want.

You might never discover the exact reason.

And that's one of the difficult parts of human relationships.

We often want explanations from people who are no longer willing to provide them.

But constantly searching for an answer can keep you emotionally attached to a situation that has already ended.

You can acknowledge that someone mattered to you without continuing to chase their attention.

You can accept that a connection was meaningful to you, even if it wasn't equally meaningful to them.

And you can recognize that sometimes, the absence of an explanation is itself information about the kind of communication that person was willing to offer.
