There's another possibility that people don't always want to consider.

Sometimes, someone genuinely loses interest.

Not because they're emotionally unavailable.

Not because they're secretly manipulating you.

Not because they're afraid of commitment.

Sometimes, their feelings simply change.

People discover incompatibilities.

Their priorities shift.

They realize that the connection isn't what they expected.

Or they gradually recognize that they don't want the same things.

Relationships are not contracts that guarantee two people will always feel the same way.

And while that reality can be uncomfortable, it's important to acknowledge it.

The problem isn't necessarily that someone no longer wants a relationship.

People are allowed to change their minds.

The problem is how they communicate that decision.

There's a considerable difference between honestly telling someone that your feelings have changed and leaving them to figure it out through silence.

One offers clarity.

The other leaves someone questioning their own worth.
