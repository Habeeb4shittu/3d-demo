Perhaps the strangest part of human connection is how quickly two people can move from feeling close to becoming strangers.

One day, someone knows the smallest details about your life.

And eventually, they become someone you hesitate to message.

But not every connection is meant to last forever.

Some people enter our lives for a season.

Some leave because circumstances change.

And others leave because they never wanted the same thing in the first place.

The important thing is not to turn every person's departure into a verdict on your worth.

Because someone choosing to walk away doesn't mean you were never worth staying for.

And sometimes, the closure you keep waiting for has to come from accepting that you may never get an explanation.
